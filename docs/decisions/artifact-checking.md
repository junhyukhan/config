# Artifact-checking — public summary

The original personal decision record has been retained verbatim in a private
archive. This page preserves its general lesson, not a new decision or a claim
about currently installed skills.

A short ambient rule can establish a disposition: verify claims against primary
artifacts rather than summaries. Concrete checks belong at their points of use:

- Cross-check surprising empty search results with another method.
- Check dates before claiming something was missed.
- Surface contradictions between a record and the underlying artifact.
- Transcribe evidence completely, or identify the subset taken.

A correctly placed instruction is not proof that it is effective. Repeated
failures should prompt review of the mechanism, not just repetition of the rule.
